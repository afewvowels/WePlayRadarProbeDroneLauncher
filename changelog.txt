Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-11-12
  Features:
    - The drone launcher can face north, east, south or west. Rotate it with R while placing, or
      press R / Shift+R over a placed launcher to turn its runway (drones inside are kept).
    - Fancy flight paths (from WePlay: Long Haul Logistics): drones roll down the runway, climb
      straight out, bank toward their target and fly a straight final approach to land.
      Can be turned off in map settings for straight-line flights.
    - Banking turns: drones roll into their turns (wings narrow, plane slides toward the inside of
      the turn). Can be turned off in map settings.
  Graphics:
    - New launcher art for all four runway directions.
  Changes:
    - The launcher and drone now get their own crafting-menu row, right after the radar's row,
      worked out after overhaul mods (Krastorio 2, Space Exploration) finish moving things.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash when rotating a placed launcher (Factorio 2.x renamed the crafter input inventory).
    - Rotating a launcher mid-launch now returns the drone that was being prepared.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-09
  Features:
    - Drone launcher: a 5x5 launch pad that sends survey drones toward the nearest uncharted land.
    - Survey drones fly out, land, and deploy into a slow-scanning radar probe, or an inflatable buoy on water.
    - Probes sweep an 8-chunk radius, then shut down. A message with a map link marks each finished sweep.
    - When everything in range is charted, drones keep flying out to refresh the map.
    - Survey drones research (after radar and engine, red + green science) and three range upgrades
      that double each level: 1024 base, then +1024, +2048, +4096 tiles.
    - Works on every planet.
  Settings:
    - Startup: launch interval, base range, first range research bonus.
    - Map: drone flight speed, show drones on the map (icon + narrow charted flight trail), landing mode (nudge / fail / risky), risky failure chance, finished-probe messages.
